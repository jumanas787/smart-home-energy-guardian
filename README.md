# smart-home-energy-guardian
A smart home energy monitoring app that tracks electricity usage by room and device, provides alerts, and helps families reduce energy consumption.

Smart Home Energy Guardian is a smart energy monitoring app designed to help families understand and reduce electricity consumption.
Families often receive high electricity bills without knowing which rooms or devices consume the most energy.
A phone app and smart-home display that help families monitor electricity usage and make better energy-saving decisions.
- Monitor electricity consumption by room.
- Track individual devices such as air conditioners, TVs, and lights.
- Send alerts when electricity usage is unusually high.
- Estimate electricity costs.
- Show daily and weekly energy usage.
- Suggest practical ways to save electricity.
- Display energy information on phones and smart-home screens.
The first version will use simulated electricity data to demonstrate the monitoring features. Real device integration is a future goal.
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
Help families identify energy waste, reduce unnecessary electricity consumption, and lower their environmental impact.
